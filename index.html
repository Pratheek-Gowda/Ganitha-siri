Ganithasiri — Science & Mathematics Exhibition

Alva's Nudisiri Virasat • January 6 – January 10

As instructed from authorities, in connection with the upcoming Alva's Nudisiri Virasat held from January 6 to January 10, student teams are required to serve as scientific volunteers and exhibit working mathematics models during the event.

Compulsory Group Size

Exact 5 Students per Group (6 Teams = 30 Total)

Each of the 6 group leaders leads a team of 5 members including themselves.

Auto-Assignment Cutoff

Sharp November 25 at 11:59 PM

Remaining unassigned members will be auto-assigned to open slots and locked.

Team Idea Submission

December 1

Every group must submit their working mathematics model idea on December 1.

Working Model Submission

December 15

Completed working mathematics models must be submitted on December 15.

Key Rules to Remember:
  • Assign accurate members to all 6 group columns before November 25, 11:59 PM.
  • Once a student is assigned to any group, they immediately vanish from the available pool for other groups.
  • Pre-assigned members under PRATHEEK (Nishith, Sant Siddappa Metagudda, Shripad) are locked.
